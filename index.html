      About Daily Reflection

      A daily reflection is when you take a little time each day to think about your experiences, feelings, actions, and thoughts. It’s like looking back at your day to understand yourself better and learn from what happened.

      For example, during a daily reflection, you might ask yourself:

      • What went well today?
      • What challenges did I face?
      • How did I feel throughout the day?
      • What can I improve tomorrow?

      People often write these reflections in a journal, but you can also do them mentally or use apps. Over time, daily reflections help you track personal growth, notice patterns in your moods or behavior, and make better choices.
